# 靜心小棧 隱私權政策 / Still Nook Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated：2026-11-23

---

## 繁體中文

「靜心小棧」（以下稱「本 App」）重視你的隱私。本政策說明本 App 會記錄哪些資料、為什麼記錄，以及你能怎麼控制。

### 我們記錄什麼
本 App **不需要帳號**，也**不蒐集**你的姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、照片、裝置識別碼或廣告識別碼。

唯一會記錄的是**匿名使用統計**，每次練習開始與結束時各送出一筆：
- 你練習的**場景**（例如「雲海日出」）
- 練習的**開始時間**，以及實際練習的**時長**（秒）
- 這次練習是否**完整結束**
- 本 App 的**版本號**
- 一組由本 App **隨機產生的匿名編號**（用來統計使用人數與回訪），以及每次練習各自的隨機編號

匿名編號存在你裝置的鑰匙圈，**不是**從你的裝置、Apple ID 或任何帳號推算出來的，所以它代表的是「這次安裝」，不是「你這個人」。你可以在「設定」→「隱私」按「重設匿名編號」，之後的記錄就會像是新的使用者。重新安裝 App 後，這組編號通常會保留。

結束時少於 10 秒的練習，不會送出「結束」記錄。

### 為什麼記錄
僅用於了解哪些場景常被使用、練習通常多長、有多少人使用與回訪，藉此**改善場景**與決定之後的開發方向。不用於廣告，不出售、不分享給第三方，也不用於追蹤你。

### 存放在哪裡
資料存放在 Apple 的 **CloudKit**（本 App 的公開資料庫）。本 App 不使用自己的伺服器，也不使用任何第三方分析工具。

### 能不能辨識是誰
**不能。** 記錄中沒有你的姓名、帳號、電子郵件或裝置識別碼。匿名編號只能讓我們知道「同一次安裝」的多筆記錄屬於同一個來源，無法得知那是誰，我們也不會把它和任何個人身分連結。我們不讀取或使用 CloudKit 在記錄上自動附帶的建立者代碼。

### 保留多久
因為記錄是匿名、無法回推到任何人，統計記錄**不設保留期限**，會持續保存用於改善場景。

### 如何停用與刪除
- **停用**：到本 App 首頁的「設定」→「隱私」，關閉「協助改善場景（匿名使用統計）」。關閉後不會再傳送任何資料；也可以按「重設匿名編號」。首次開啟時也可以直接選「關閉統計」。
- **重設編號**：同一處的「重設匿名編號」會換一組新的隨機編號，之前的記錄不再與你的安裝關聯。
- **刪除**：因為記錄是匿名的，我們無法找出哪幾筆屬於你，所以無法單獨刪除。停用後不會再新增記錄；既有的匿名記錄無法回推到你。重設匿名編號不會刪除舊記錄，只是讓之後的記錄不再與舊的相連。

### 儲存在你裝置上的資料
你的設定、我的最愛與最近看過的場景只存在你的裝置，不會傳送給我們。

### 兒童
本 App 不針對特定年齡層蒐集個人資料；因為不記錄任何個人資料，對兒童也一樣。

### 政策變更
若日後蒐集的內容改變，我們會更新本政策並更新上方日期。

## English

Still Nook ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app records, why, and how you can control it.

### What we record
The app has **no accounts** and does **not collect** your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

The only thing recorded is **anonymous usage statistics**, one entry when a session starts and one when it ends:
- the **scene** you practiced (for example "Sea of Clouds")
- the session's **start time** and how long you practiced, in **seconds**
- whether the session was **completed**
- the app's **version number**
- a **random anonymous ID** generated by the app (used to count users and returning users), plus a random ID for each session

The anonymous ID is kept in your device's Keychain. It is **not** derived from your device, Apple ID or any account, so it stands for "this installation", not "you". In Settings → Privacy you can tap "Reset anonymous ID", after which later entries look like a new user. The ID usually survives reinstalling the app.

Sessions shorter than 10 seconds at the end do not send an "end" entry.

### Why
Only to learn which scenes are used, how long sessions usually last, and how many people use and return to the app, so we can **improve the scenes** and decide what to build next. It is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with third parties, and is not used to track you.

### Where it is stored
In Apple's **CloudKit** (the app's public database). The app runs no servers of its own and uses no third-party analytics.

### Can it identify you?
**No.** Entries contain no name, account, email or device identifier. The anonymous ID only lets us tell that several entries come from the same installation, not who that is, and we never link it to any personal identity. We do not read or use the creator code CloudKit attaches to records automatically.

### How long we keep it
Because entries are anonymous and cannot be traced to anyone, statistics have **no retention limit** and are kept to keep improving the scenes.

### How to turn it off and delete it
- **Turn off**: on the app's home screen, go to Settings → Privacy and switch off "Help improve the scenes (anonymous usage stats)". Nothing is sent once it is off; you can also reset the anonymous ID. You can also choose "Turn Off Stats" in the notice shown on first launch.
- **Reset ID**: "Reset Anonymous ID" in the same place generates a new random ID, so earlier entries are no longer associated with your installation.
- **Delete**: entries are anonymous, so we cannot tell which ones are yours and cannot delete them individually. After you turn it off, no new entries are added; existing anonymous entries cannot be traced back to you. Resetting the ID does not delete old entries; it only stops later entries from connecting to them.

### Data on your device
Your settings, favorites and recently viewed scenes stay on your device and are never sent to us.

### Children
The app does not collect personal data from anyone, children included, because it records none.

### Changes
If what we record changes, we will update this policy and the date above.
