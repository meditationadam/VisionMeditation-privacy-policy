支援 / Support

有問題、回報錯誤或建議，請寄信到 meditationadam@gmail.com，我們通常在幾個工作天內回覆。
For questions, bug reports or suggestions, email meditationadam@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

回報問題時請附上：場景名稱、visionOS 版本、發生了什麼事。
When reporting an issue, please include the scene name, your visionOS version, and what happened.

常見問題

進入場景後畫面一片黑？

場景是即時產生的，載入後前面幾十秒材質可能還沒就緒，請稍等片刻。若一直沒有恢復，請回到首頁重新進入場景，或重新啟動 App。

怎麼隱藏我不想看到的場景？

可以在首頁隱藏場景，之後不會出現在列表中。也能把常用的場景加入我的最愛。

需要什麼裝置？

Apple Vision Pro，並升級到 visionOS 26 或更新版本。

怎麼關閉匿名使用統計？

首頁「設定」→「隱私」，關閉「協助改善場景（匿名使用統計）」。也可以按「重設匿名編號」。詳見隱私權政策。

需要帳號或網路嗎？

不需要帳號。場景與聲音都在裝置上產生，只有匿名統計（可關閉）會用到網路。

FAQ

The scene is black after I enter it.

Scenes are generated in real time, and materials may take a few seconds to settle after loading. If it stays dark, go back to Home and re-enter the scene, or relaunch the app.

How do I hide scenes I don't want to see?

You can hide scenes from the home screen so they no longer appear in the list. You can also add favorites.

What do I need?

An Apple Vision Pro running visionOS 26 or later.

How do I turn off anonymous usage stats?

Home → Settings → Privacy → switch off "Help improve the scenes (anonymous usage stats)". You can also reset the anonymous ID. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account. Scenes and sound are generated on-device; only the optional anonymous stats use the network.
